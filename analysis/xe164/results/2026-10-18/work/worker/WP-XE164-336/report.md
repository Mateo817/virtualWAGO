# WP-XE164-336 — independent audit of WP324 callback contracts

**VERIFIZIERT**, within the pinned source scope: the seven `libpackbus.so.1.0.1` bodies contain 415 words and 11 separate pool words; the separately bounded ReadStartGate has 26 words plus one literal. Raw word bytes bind to the pinned ELF. The literal arithmetic in each of the seven methods and the optional helper yields image-relative ELF cell `0x44420` (analysis/Ghidra `0x54420`). The seven method bodies contain 32 encoded BL instructions total: 31 imported PLT calls and the ReadStart-to-ReadStartGate local call. The optional helper adds four PLT calls. These facts establish local source relationships only.

**PASS**: all WP324 source pins and its manifest-listed outputs match; its default validator ran with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`, passed, and left the WP324 manifest-listed inventory unchanged. WP336 independently checked raw instruction bytes, A32 BL encodings and decoded targets against the callsite ledger, PLT target/relocation ordering, literal arithmetic, and selected arithmetic/control words. No WP324 files were edited or regenerated. This review reuses WP320’s retained REA records as its acquisition baseline and treats WP324 matrices as comparison artifacts; it independently rechecks the raw words and highlighted source routes. It does not reuse a decoder, emulate the ELF, or claim runtime behavior.

The seven body counts, call totals, cold global routes and register captures are recorded in `method-matrix.tsv`. `plt-review.tsv` records every PLT symbol’s callsite count. The 32 main BL instructions decode to their recorded branch targets; conditional BLS/BCC/BCS branches are not counted as calls. The optional helper call targets also map by `.plt` entry to `pthread_rwlock_rdlock`, `MISC_threads_acquire_lock`, `SSC_access_handle_rx_buffer`, and `pthread_rwlock_unlock`. PLT names are target labels, not prototypes or implementation contracts.

Here `G` denotes the literal-derived cell address, not a nullable pointer. The source loads `S=[G]` and tests that loaded word. For ReadEnd, the exact non-error predicate is `S!=0 && [S]!=0 && [S+4]!=0`; there is no separate null/readability test on address G. WriteStart likewise checks loaded S first, then `[S]` and `[S+4]` after its rdlock call. WP324’s report sentence saying “G, S, [S], or [S+4]” is imprecise and repeats the loaded-cell test; the source-derived predicate is as stated here. This does not add a guard for an unreadable or invalid G address.

ReadStart’s helper-zero path executes the caller’s `SUBS r4,r0,#0`, sets Z, takes BEQ to the `clock_gettime` call, and later restores the captured zero to r0 if that call returns and r4 survives. The separately sourced helper’s `S=[G]==0` route returns `0xffffffff`; the caller’s SUBS clears Z, skips the clock call, and returns the captured word. Other helper outcomes are opaque. The selected cold loaded-word routes for the remaining bodies return their locally encoded `0xffffffff` without reaching a PLT call; DiagGetDeviceState can make its optional earlier `memset` call when entry r1 is nonzero before testing its output address and loaded global word.

The ReadBytes source captures entry r0/r1/r2 in r5/r6/r8; WriteBytes captures them in r5/r6/r7. Those are register aliases only. Both bodies use 32-bit ADD and unsigned comparisons. ReadBytes computes `E=U32(X+Y)`, `M=min_unsigned([S+0xac]>>3,Q0>>3)`, and `F=A+min_unsigned(U32([S+0xa8]+7)>>3,U32(Q1+7)>>3)` with `A=[S+0xa4]`. WriteBytes computes `E=U32(X+Y)`, `M=min_unsigned([S+0xb8]>>3,Q0>>3)`, and `F=B+min_unsigned(U32([S+0xb4]+7)>>3,U32(Q1+7)>>3)` with `B=[S+0xb0]`. `branch-review.tsv` and `segment-call-review.tsv` record source route gates and all 18 ReadBytes/WriteBytes imported call vectors; these are source-defined segment calculations, not a claim of general API-level range correctness.

ReadBytes alone has two `memset`-named call sites (`0x1ef5c`, `0x1f01c`) on the selected segmented routes; WriteBytes has none. Imported call effects remain opaque. A complete post-call premise for either ReadBytes memset continuation must preserve the values that its following path actually uses: `r4=F`, `r5=X`, `r10=E`, `r11=A`, needed `r7=S` and `r8=Z`, the stack frame and saved query words. If the selected continuation reloads object state, the global cell word `S=[G]`, required state words, and a valid `[S+4]` object pointer must also remain available; these callees’ memory effects are opaque. WP324’s memset call-vector row names a subset (`r5/r6/r7/r8` plus frame/local words); this review makes the additional live `r4/r10/r11` and conditional global/state-memory requirements explicit and does not treat a symbol name as proof they survive. The length-query routes require the called routine to populate Q0/Q1/Q2 at the passed stack addresses and the source’s base register to remain usable for the following current-global reload. The WriteBytes first SSC_Put continuation separately depends on live `r4/r5/r7/r8/r9`, its frame/output words and any global/state words that a selected later segment reloads. These are conditional premises, not ABI claims.

DiagGetDeviceState stores numeric words 3, 0, 2 or 1 through the current entry-r2 address on its corresponding source paths, then reaches the shared epilogue with r0 equal to zero on those paths. The null-output/global error route encodes `0xffffffff`, and can occur after the optional initial memset. These are numeric outputs and local status flow; they do not establish an enum, API meaning, or safety contract.
