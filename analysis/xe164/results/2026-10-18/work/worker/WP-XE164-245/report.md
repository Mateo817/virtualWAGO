## Method and limits

The CFG edge inventory includes sequential edges, direct conditional/unconditional branch targets and fallthrough, opaque direct/indirect-call return edges, each verified table-case target, each table guard's default fallthrough, register BX transfers, stack PC restores, and unresolved indirect/computed PC transfers. Raw table addresses from all 17 assigned dispatch rows are removed before edge discovery. Register-indirect calls are not traversed: every call clobbers all tracked GPR and memory facts for the continuation. BX is a tail/indirect branch class, not conflated with a BLX call. No calling convention is assumed.

For each `ldr <reg>,[<reg>,#32]` site the candidate search carries the loaded value through decoded CFG edges, plain register `mov` copies, and conditional-write alternatives. Any intervening write to a carrier register or opaque call kills that copy. Each reported candidate has a concrete address path; its branch conditions are not solver-proved. Per-call reaching definitions use a finite site-based abstract interpretation over the six marker seeds, exact REA procedure entries, and conservative direct/table destinations. Every such entry starts with unknown registers. `r0` definitions are classified separately as constant 15, other constants, computed, memory-loaded, incoming-register, or opaque-call-clobber. A merged reaching set is not itself a concrete joint-path proof; candidate witness and per-call limit fields keep that distinction visible. Unknown r0 is never classified as unequal to 15.

`r0-definition-sites.tsv` inventories 6408 explicit r0 writes and opaque direct/indirect calls that may clobber r0. Its `may_reaching_blx_calls` links come from merged reaching-definition tokens and are not joint-path proofs. `selector15-lane.tsv` binds the sole explicit in-scope immediate `r0=15` write at 0x176e58 to its same-condition `BLE` and the exact `0x176eac` LDM/PC epilogue. The three REA-seeded entries plus six bounded local lanes give all 60 BLX rows a local abstract state; no row has a classified constant-15 r0 token. The six local lanes do not establish function ownership or a whole-function selector exclusion. `unseeded-blx-review.tsv` byte-binds their exact short instruction lanes and names the REA resolution result. `unresolved-gaps.tsv` records undefined words, unresolved PC transfers, out-of-scope/unmapped direct edges, indirect targets, unresolved ownership, and every +0x20 load's unmodeled spill/reload/alias uncertainty. Unsupported data/control effects and path-condition feasibility remain explicit limitations. The results are static observations of this exact ELF/listing only.
