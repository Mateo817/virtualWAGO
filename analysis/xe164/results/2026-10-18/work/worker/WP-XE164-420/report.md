**OFFEN — one read-set omission:** WP419 says that after `ioctl` the only register consumed by the source decision is current r4, and its contract lists only r4. However, the actual next instruction in both bodies is `CMP r0,r4` (`0xe1500004`, at ELF PCs `0xab34` and `0xad60`). It consumes the returned r0 as well as r4 to set flags for the following `BGE`. WP419's traces correctly carry the modeled ioctl return in r0 and their branches follow that value; the gap is in the claimed explicit read-set contract/prose, not the replay result. A corrected contract should list both r0 (the call's returned value) and r4 (the comparison operand), with the result flags consumed by the next branch. No assertion is made here about what a real ioctl implementation returns or preserves.

Its saved-frame checks are appropriately limited to the modeled SP-relative words and do not establish ABI compliance.
